Quality & Sourcing

Trust Is Built
on Details.

A guide to how buyers can evaluate our Ceylon cinnamon — and the documentation you can request from us before, and with, every order.

Request Documents

Seven questions every serious buyer should ask.

We would rather you ask them of us than assume. Where information is not yet available, we will tell you plainly.

  1. 01

    Product origin

    Each product is identified as Ceylon cinnamon (Cinnamomum verum) of Sri Lankan origin. Supporting origin documentation can be discussed per shipment.

    Ask for: origin details, certificate of origin

  2. 02

    Product identification & specifications

    Grades, dimensions, moisture and other parameters are stated per product and per lot — never implied by a label. Specification sheets are shared on request.

    Ask for: product specification sheet

  3. 03

    Sourcing & traceability

    Where traceability information is available — such as sourcing region or lot references — we share it. Where it is not, we say so rather than fill the gap with assumptions.

    Ask for: lot and sourcing information, where available

  4. 04

    Laboratory testing & food-safety documents

    Appropriate testing depends on your market and application. Available test reports and food-safety documentation can be requested; specific tests can be discussed for your requirements.

    Ask for: available test reports, certificate of analysis

  5. 05

    Packaging & storage

    Cinnamon is sensitive to moisture, light and odour. Packaging formats, inner liners and recommended storage conditions are agreed in advance with each buyer.

    Ask for: packaging specification, storage guidance

  6. 06

    Export documentation

    Typical export shipments may involve documents such as a commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and phytosanitary certificate. Requirements vary by destination and are confirmed per order.

    Ask for: export document list for your country

  7. 07

    Your quality requirements

    Share your own specification, testing protocol or retailer standard. We will review it and tell you honestly which requirements can be met and how.

    Send us: your buyer specification
