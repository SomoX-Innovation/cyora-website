The House of CYORA

Some Stories Begin
With a Place.

Ours begins on a tropical island, where warm rain, coastal breezes and patient hands have shaped one of the world’s most treasured spices.

Ceylon cinnamon carries the character of its origin — delicate, sweetly aromatic and layered — and it is cherished for exactly that reason. At CYORA, we believe a spice this distinctive deserves to be presented with the same care that goes into making it.

That is why we focus on what serious buyers ask about first: a clear origin, careful sourcing, consistent product, honest specifications and a responsible way of doing business. Beautiful presentation matters. Trust matters more.

Origin

Born From the
Land of Ceylon.

For centuries, the name Ceylon has travelled the world alongside cinnamon. Merchants crossed oceans for the fragrant quills of this island, and today true cinnamon — Cinnamomum verum — remains closely tied to Sri Lanka’s identity.

It grows in the island’s humid lowlands, where the trees are cut back so that slender new shoots rise from the base. The bark is scraped, its delicate inner layer eased free, and the thin sheets are layered and rolled into quills that dry slowly in the shade.

It is quiet, skilled work, and every quill carries a little of it. From those fields to a buyer’s warehouse on the other side of the world, our role is to keep that story intact.
